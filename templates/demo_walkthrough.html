Demo walkthrough

Follow one fictional supplier case through all three workspaces.

{{ 'Open your workspace →' if g.user else 'Sign in →' }}

Use your existing applicant, reviewer and administrator accounts. For a fresh installation, follow START_HERE.md and ADMIN_GUIDE.md in the project folder first. Sign out before switching roles, or use separate browser profiles.

  1. 01 · Applicant

    Submit a registration

    Open New registration. Enter a fictional company such as Gulf Trading LLC, select Office supplies, and use a sample reference such as DEMO-LIC-104 with a future expiry date.

    Save the draft. Upload sample-trade-licence.pdf and sample-company-profile.pdf from sample-documents. Click Submit for demo review and keep the GV- reference.

  2. 02 · Administrator

    Assign the case

    Find the GV- reference in Administration. Under Reviewer assignment, choose a reviewer and click Save assignment. Open Reviewer workload to see it in that reviewer’s open cases.

  3. 03 · Reviewer

    Review and approve

    Open the assigned case from Review queue and click Start review. Check the company details and both PDFs, tick all four review checks, then click Save checklist and Approve.

    The case should now show Approved. Click Open demo approval summary to see the printable record.

  4. 04 · Applicant

    Renew the approval

    Return to the approved case and click Start renewal. Save with a later expiry. Click Compare renewal details: if you changed only the expiry, it should show 1 of 4 fields changed.

    Upload the sample PDFs to this renewal and submit it for review. It has its own GV- reference.

  5. 05 · Administrator and reviewer

    Check the approval handover

    While the renewal is pending, Approved suppliers still shows the original approval. Assign and approve the renewal using the same review steps. The directory should then show the renewal.

    Search Activity log by the renewal reference to trace its history. The original approval remains available in Administration.
